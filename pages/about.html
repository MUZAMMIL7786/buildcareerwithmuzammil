Meet Our Founder

From Corporate Insider to Career Catalyst

Hi, I'm Rajesh Kumar, and I've spent the last 8 years on both sides of the hiring table—as a senior recruiter at TCS and Infosys, and now as the founder of Build Career With Muzammil.

During my corporate years, I reviewed over 50,000 applications and conducted 2,000+ interviews. I saw brilliant graduates with stellar academics get rejected—not because they lacked talent, but because they didn't understand the hidden rules of the job market.

"I realized that the difference between getting hired and getting ignored wasn't about qualifications—it was about strategy, positioning, and insider connections. That's why I founded Build Career With Muzammil."

Today, I leverage my extensive network of 500+ hiring managers, recruiters, and industry leaders to give fresh graduates the unfair advantage they need. We don't just teach job search tactics—we open doors that most candidates don't even know exist.
